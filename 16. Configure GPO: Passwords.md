We will use Group Policies to strengthen security. <br> <br>

Check current password policy
```powershell
Get-ADDefaultDomainPasswordPolicy
```

Implement traditional enterprise-style baseline password policy <br>
Note, Modern enterprise don't have password expiration now. They use MFA and strong account-lockout/smart-lockout controls.
```powershell
Set-ADDefaultDomainPasswordPolicy `
    -Identity "adlab.test" `
    -ComplexityEnabled $true `
    -MinPasswordLength 12 `
    -PasswordHistoryCount 24 `
    -MinPasswordAge (New-TimeSpan -Days 1) `
    -MaxPasswordAge (New-TimeSpan -Days 90) `
    -ReversibleEncryptionEnabled $false
```

Verify
```powershell
Get-ADDefaultDomainPasswordPolicy |
    Select-Object ComplexityEnabled,
                  MinPasswordLength,
                  PasswordHistoryCount,
                  MinPasswordAge,
                  MaxPasswordAge,
                  ReversibleEncryptionEnabled
```

Implement Lockout policy
```powershell
Set-ADDefaultDomainPasswordPolicy `
    -Identity "adlab.test" `
    -LockoutThreshold 5 `
    -LockoutDuration (New-TimeSpan -Minutes 15) `
    -LockoutObservationWindow (New-TimeSpan -Minutes 15)
```

Verify
```powershell
Get-ADDefaultDomainPasswordPolicy -Identity "adlab.test" |
    Select-Object `
        ComplexityEnabled,
        MinPasswordLength,
        PasswordHistoryCount,
        MinPasswordAge,
        MaxPasswordAge,
        ReversibleEncryptionEnabled,
        LockoutThreshold,
        LockoutDuration,
        LockoutObservationWindow
```
